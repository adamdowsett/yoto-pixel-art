Yoto Pixel Art — 16×16

Yoto Pixel Art

Click a colour, then click a pixel to paint it. Yoto doesn't enforce a fixed palette technically — any PNG works — but a small, high-contrast palette reads best at 16 pixels. Export when it looks right at 1×.
